BAUVIZ (Visualization in Architecture, Engineering & Construction) brings together researchers and practitioners from visualization, architecture, engineering, and construction to explore how visual analytics, immersive methods, and human–AI collaboration can support the digital transformation of the built environment. Spanning the full lifecycle of buildings and urban systems, from early design and simulation through construction, operation, and reuse, the workshop serves as a forum for sharing new techniques, identifying emerging challenges, and building interdisciplinary collaborations within the EuroVis community.
