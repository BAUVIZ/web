Scope

The Architecture, Engineering, and Construction (AEC) sector is undergoing a major digital transformation. Computational design and engineering methods, cyber-physical fabrication and construction processes, and sensor-enhanced operation and maintenance of the built environment are generating large amounts of heterogeneous and dynamic data. Data visualization and visual analytics have become central components of data-intensive workflows in many scientific and engineering disciplines.

BAUVIZ provides a forum for discussing visual analytics, immersive visualization, engineering simulations, digital fabrication, and human–AI collaboration across the full lifecycle of buildings and urban systems — from early-stage design and engineering analysis to fabrication, construction, operation, maintenance, disassembly, and reuse. By fostering interdisciplinary exchange, the workshop aims to identify emerging challenges, inspire new collaborations, and strengthen the presence of built-environment visualization research within the EuroVis community.

Topics of Interest

We seek contributions that advance the development and application of visualization, visual analytics, and human-centered data exploration techniques across the lifecycle of the built environment. Topics include, but are not limited to, the following areas:

Architecture & Computational Design

  • Generative and computational design visualization
  • Design space exploration
  • Architectural performance visualization
  • Explainable AI for design workflows
  • AI copilots for architectural design
  • Urban visualization and Geographic Information Systems (GIS)

Engineering & Simulation

  • Engineering simulation visualization
  • Uncertainty visualization
  • Optimization and sensitivity analysis
  • Comparative design evaluation
  • Human-in-the-loop engineering workflows
  • Life-cycle assessment and embodied carbon visualization

Digital Fabrication & Construction

  • Construction planning and monitoring
  • Reality capture and point-cloud visualization
  • Robotic fabrication and construction
  • Circular construction
  • Construction site safety and workforce analytics
  • Renovation and retrofitting workflows
  • Material logistics analytics

BIM & Digital Twins

  • BIM visualization and interaction
  • Semantic and interoperable data
  • Digital twin analytics
  • Sensor and IoT data
  • Scalable rendering of large BIM and infrastructure models

Human–Building Interaction & Facility Management

  • Indoor environmental quality and occupant comfort
  • Building performance monitoring and fault detection
  • Energy consumption and smart-meter analytics
  • Facility management and dashboards
  • Structural health monitoring and asset condition assessment

Immersive & Collaborative Visualization

  • Virtual and augmented reality for AEC
  • Immersive analytics for building and site data
  • On-site AR guidance for construction and maintenance
  • Collaborative visualization environments
  • Embodied interaction and collaboration
  • Stakeholder communication and education

Submissions

We invite the following contribution types, all of which must be related to visualization in architecture, engineering, and construction:

  • Short research papers — original work in the EuroVis short-paper format (up to 4 pages in two-column Eurographics style, excluding references; abstract of max. 150 words).
  • Position / vision papers — to foster discussion and envision future directions in built-environment visualization.
  • Industrial experience reports — lessons learned from deploying visualization in real AEC workflows.
  • Demonstrations — working systems, tools, or interactive artifacts.
  • Work-in-progress contributions — early-stage results open to critique.
Submission system: All contributions will be handled through the Precision Conference System (PCS), via EuroVis 2027 → “BAUVIZ: Visualization in Architecture, Engineering & Construction” track. Please read and follow the EuroVis 2027 author guidelines and the Eurographics author guidelines prior to submission.

Submissions will be evaluated through peer review with at least two IPC members and one paper chair; external reviewers will be assigned on demand if required (e.g., emergency reviews). Please follow the Eurographics policy on the use of generative AI tools in published work.

Workshop Format

BAUVIZ is a half-day workshop. We plan a format that fosters individual paper presentations as thought-provoking seeds, followed by an interactive discussion with all participants. We will invite a keynote speaker from one of the fields of digital twins, computational design, visual analytics, explainable AI, smart infrastructure, or construction informatics.

Tentative schedule:

  • Introduction & Keynote (45 min) — opener, possibly attracting additional audience from the main conference.
  • Paper Presentations (90 min) — presentations and Q&A of accepted short papers (we expect 6–10; length adjusted accordingly).
  • Interactive Panel Discussion (45 min) — moderated discussion between authors, invited guests, and the audience.
  • Vision & Roadmap Session (45 min) — research directions and the future of this workshop; potentially fused with the panel.

Target audience: visualization and visual-analytics researchers; computer-graphics and HCI researchers; architects and computational designers; AEC practitioners; civil, structural, and construction engineers; BIM and digital-twin experts; urban-informatics researchers. We expect 20–40 participants drawn from EuroVis and the broader AEC communities.
